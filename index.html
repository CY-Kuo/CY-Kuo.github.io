Selected Projects

Connector-Aware Robotic Assembly 🤖

Manuscript under review.

A geometry-aware robotic assembly framework that reasons from individual CAD/mesh parts to generate assembly plans, validate assembly sequences and motions in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and transfer validated results to a physical robot system.

Project

MAPS: Manual-Guided Assembly-Part Segmentation 👁️

The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology, 2026

A manual-guided vision framework for segmenting assembly parts under clutter and occlusion. It combines a camera image with the aligned 2D assembly-manual step as structural guidance, without requiring CAD or depth at deployment.
